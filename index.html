ABOUT

The Printmaking Lab

The Printmaking Lab is an artist-led platform based in Sri Lanka dedicated to printmaking, contemporary art, research, exhibitions, education and collaboration.

The Lab develops projects that connect artists, ideas, materials and communities through printmaking and expanded artistic practices.

Personal growth and social benefit.
