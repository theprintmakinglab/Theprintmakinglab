Art · Land · Material

The Idea

Where art, cultivation and material transformation meet.

At The Printmaking Lab, art and organic farming grow from the same soil. Compost, fallen leaves, discarded textiles, plants, soil and living processes become materials for observation, experimentation and artistic practice.

01 · The Project

Art · Ecology · Printmaking

A living field of slow transformations.

Organic farming here is not only food production. It is a practice of care and a way of observing transformation through soil, microorganisms, roots, moisture and time.

The project brings ecological processes and printmaking into a shared living environment, connecting the studio with rural and forest landscapes.

02 · Workshops

Learn by making.

Participants are invited to work directly with materials, plants, soil and artistic processes. Workshops combine practical experience with creative experimentation.

03 · Independent Practice

From learning to making independently.

The project encourages participants to move beyond guided activities, develop their own ideas and continue experimenting with materials and processes independently.

The aim is not simply to teach a technique, but to create conditions for observation, experimentation, curiosity and self-directed practice.

“A space where creativity, ecology and community support one another.”

04 · The Land

A place to work, observe and grow.

The rural landscape becomes part of the artistic process — a place where cultivation, material research, workshops and creative practice can happen together.
